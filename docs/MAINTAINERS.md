## Admission and removal

Maintainer admission requires a documented decision by the current maintainer,
evidence of sustained review or implementation work, agreement to the
security and DCO policies, and least-privilege access. Reviewer access may be
granted for a narrower path scope before maintainer access.

Permission escalation must name the capability, reason, approver, and expiry
or review point. Inactive or conflicted maintainers may have access reduced or
removed by the remaining active maintainer, with security-sensitive credentials
rotated as needed. If no active maintainer remains, repository administration,
advisory handling, and release authority must be transferred through the
available GitHub/PyPI recovery process before a release is made.
