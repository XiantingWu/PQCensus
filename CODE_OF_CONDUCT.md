# PQCensus Code of Conduct

PQCensus is committed to a welcoming, respectful, technically rigorous community. Participants are expected to communicate constructively, assume good faith while evaluating evidence critically, and protect private information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, malicious code, or publishing another person's private information. Maintainers may moderate discussions, reject contributions, remove content, or restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not include secrets, credentials, private source, or other sensitive material in a report.

This is the project's own code of conduct; it does not claim adoption of the Contributor Covenant or another external governance standard.
